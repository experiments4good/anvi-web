Kapha is the dosha of structure and steadiness. Welch lists its qualities as oily, cool, heavy, slow, smooth, soft and stable, so the advice for Kapha is to stay light, warm and active. Welch puts it plainly: activity is one of the best medicines for Kapha. If another dosha is clearly out of balance right now, Lad's advice is to balance that one first.

## Food

- Favor light, warm, dry foods with pungent, bitter and astringent tastes, in moderate portions.
- Good choices, from Lad's chart: barley, buckwheat, millet and rye, with basmati rice in moderation; garlic, onions, peppers, cabbage and leafy greens; apples, berries, pears and pomegranates; black-eyed peas. Nearly all spices suit Kapha.
- Go easy on wheat, white and brown rice, cucumber, sweet potatoes, bananas, avocado, coconut, dates, cow's milk, cheese, ice cream, salt and cold drinks.
- Stick to three meals, and make dinner your lightest.
