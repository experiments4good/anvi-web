Pitta and Kapha both lead your constitution. Ayurvedic teacher Dr. David Frawley describes two-dosha types as having both doshas in roughly equal amounts, and he gives one set of guidance for each pair, so it applies whichever dosha is named first. He describes Pitta as determined and Kapha as steady. What the two share is an oily, damp quality, so the core advice for this type is to stay dry and light.

## What to balance right now

Three established teachers agree on where to start. Dr. Vasant Lad of the Ayurvedic Institute and Dr. David Frawley both say to first balance whichever dosha is out of balance right now. When neither one stands out, Frawley and Dr. Claudia Welch both say to go by the time of year. Frawley balances Pitta from about May 1 to November 1 and Kapha from about November 1 to May 1. <em class="now" data-pair="pitta-kapha" hidden>Right now that means balancing Pitta, through Nov 1.</em>

## Food

- Favor pungent, bitter and astringent tastes, like cumin, dark leafy greens and apples.
- Use aromatic spices, not hot ones: ginger, cinnamon, cloves, cardamom and turmeric.
- Go easy on oily, fried and heavy foods.
- Build meals around fresh green vegetables. Basmati rice is a balanced grain for you, in moderation in the Kapha months.
- Go lighter on bread, pasta and rich desserts, and on dairy in winter and spring.
- Go easy on coffee and alcohol, and keep portions moderate.
- Make lunch your biggest meal and dinner your lightest.

## Your daily routine

| | May 1 to Nov 1 (balancing Pitta) | Nov 1 to May 1 (balancing Kapha) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wake up | Around sunrise | Before sunrise, by about 6 |
| Oil massage | Coconut or sunflower oil | Sesame oil |
| Move | Walking or yoga, in the cooler morning hours | Brisk exercise or Sun Salutations, before 10 |

## Mind and mood

- Hold plans and outcomes loosely. Frawley advises this type to stay detached and avoid over-involvement.
- If you're upset, pause before you eat.

## By season

- **Fall:** Favor pungent, bitter and astringent tastes, like cumin, dark leafy greens and apples, with aromatic spices, not hot ones. Go easy on oily, fried and heavy foods, build meals around fresh green vegetables, and take a brisk walk in the morning.
- **Summer:** Favor bitter and astringent tastes, like dark leafy greens, apples and bean sprouts. Stay out of the strongest sun from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., and take your walk in the cool of the morning.
- **Winter and spring:** Favor pungent, bitter and astringent tastes, and go lighter on bread, pasta, rich desserts and dairy. Get up early and move before 10 a.m., with a brisk walk or Sun Salutations.
