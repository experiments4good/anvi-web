Vata is the dosha of movement. Welch lists its qualities as dry, light, cool, rough, subtle and mobile, so the advice for Vata is to stay warm, steady and nourished. If another dosha is clearly out of balance right now, Lad's advice is to balance that one first, then come back to Vata.

## Food

- Favor warm, freshly cooked meals like soups, stews and one-pot dishes, with sweet, sour and salty tastes.
- Good choices, from Lad's chart: rice, quinoa, cooked oats; beets, carrots, sweet potatoes, asparagus and zucchini, cooked; sweet fruits like mangoes, berries, cooked apples and avocado; ghee. Nearly all spices suit Vata.
- Go easy on dry or puffed cereals, raw vegetables, broccoli, kale, raw apples, dried fruit, coffee and iced drinks.
- Keep your meals at the same times each day, and make lunch your biggest meal.
