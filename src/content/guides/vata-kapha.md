Vata and Kapha both lead your constitution. Frawley gives one set of guidance for the pair, whichever is named first. What the two share is cold, so his core advice is to eat warm food and stay warm. First balance whichever dosha is out of balance right now (Lad, Frawley). Otherwise go by the time of year: Frawley balances Vata from about June 21 to December 21, and Kapha from about December 21 to June 21. Welch's seasons overlap in winter: Vata in fall and winter, Kapha in winter and spring. <em class="now" data-pair="vata-kapha" hidden>Right now that means balancing Vata, through Dec 21.</em>

## Food

- Favor warm, cooked food with sour, salty and pungent tastes, like lemon, miso and ginger.
- Spice generously. Frawley says most spices suit this type, even hot ones: ginger, black pepper, mustard, cardamom, cinnamon and a little chili.
- Sip warm drinks, not cold ones.
- In the Kapha months, lighten up: go easy on heavy, oily and cold foods and on dairy, and eat plenty of fresh greens.

## Your daily routine

| | Jun 21 to Dec 21 (balancing Vata) | Dec 21 to Jun 21 (balancing Kapha) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wake up | Around sunrise, at the same time every day | Before sunrise, by about 6 |
| Oil massage | Warm sesame oil | Warm sesame oil, with a brisk massage |
| Move | Gentle exercise like walking or yoga | Vigorous: a brisk walk, a bike ride or Sun Salutations, before 10 |

**Mind and mood:** Frawley advises staying close to people who support you and building a sense of warmth.
