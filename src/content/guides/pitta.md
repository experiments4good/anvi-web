Pitta is the dosha of heat and transformation. Welch lists its qualities as oily, sharp, hot, light and liquid, so the advice for Pitta is to stay cool and moderate. If another dosha is clearly out of balance right now, Lad's advice is to balance that one first.

## Food

- Favor fresh, cooling foods with sweet, bitter and astringent tastes.
- Good choices, from Lad's chart: basmati rice, barley, quinoa; asparagus, broccoli, cabbage, cucumber, leafy greens and cilantro; sweet apples, coconut, melons, watermelon and pomegranates; milk and ghee. Cooling spices: cumin, coriander, fennel, fresh ginger, mint and turmeric.
- Go easy on garlic, raw onions, hot peppers, raw tomatoes, sour fruits like lemons and grapefruit, hard cheese, fried and very salty foods, coffee and alcohol.
- Keep portions moderate, and make lunch your biggest meal.

## Your daily routine

| Wake up | Oil massage | Move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Around sunrise | Coconut or sunflower oil | Walking or yoga, in the cooler morning hours |

**Mind and mood:** Welch advises Pitta to let go of the urge to control outcomes. If you're upset, pause before you eat.

**By season:** Summer is your season to stay cool: stay out of the sun from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. and favor cucumber, zucchini and mint. In fall, winter and spring, keep to sweet, bitter and astringent tastes and moderate portions.
