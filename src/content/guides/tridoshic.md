Tridoshic means Vata, Pitta and Kapha are roughly equal in your constitution. Lad notes this is the least common result. Gran's advice is to follow the season and keep a regular routine. Welch agrees on the seasons: Vata in late fall and early winter, Pitta in late spring and summer, Kapha in late winter and spring. As with every type, if one dosha is clearly out of balance right now, balance that one first (Lad).

## Food

- Include all six tastes across the day: sweet (carrots), sour (lemon), salty (olives), pungent (ginger), bitter (dark leafy greens) and astringent (apples).
- Choose fresh, whole foods over processed ones. Basmati rice is the most balanced grain for every type (Gran).
- Go easy on caffeine and alcohol, and relax for a few minutes before you eat.
- Then lean with the season: warm soups and stews in fall and early winter, fresh fruit, greens and plenty of fluids in summer, and lighter meals with less dairy and sweets in late winter and spring.
