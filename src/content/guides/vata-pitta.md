Vata and Pitta both lead your constitution. Frawley gives one set of guidance for the pair, whichever is named first. What the two share is lightness, so his core advice is to stay grounded and steady. First balance whichever dosha is out of balance right now (Lad, Frawley). Otherwise go by the time of year: Frawley balances Pitta from about March 21 to September 21, and Vata from about September 21 to March 21. Welch's seasons line up: Vata in fall and winter, Pitta in spring and summer. <em class="now" data-pair="vata-pitta" hidden>Right now that means balancing Vata, through Mar 20.</em>

Banyan gives different advice when more than one dosha feels out of balance at once: start with Vata, through gentle movement, easy-to-digest food and a steady routine, until you can see a practitioner. A [NAMA-certified Ayurvedic practitioner](https://ayurveda.memberclicks.net/directory-find-a-professional#/) can tell you which to work on first.

## Food

- In the Vata months: warm, cooked meals with sweet, sour and salty tastes, like carrots, yogurt and miso.
- In the Pitta months: sweet, cooling foods like cucumber, zucchini and summer squash.
- All year: mild spices only, like coriander, cardamom, cloves and turmeric, and nothing too hot.
- Where the teachers differ: Frawley lists sweet, sour and salty for this type all year. Lad and Welch both say sour and salty heat Pitta, so in the Pitta months this page keeps to sweet tastes and goes easy on sour and salty.
