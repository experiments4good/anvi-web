title: Delete your Anvi account
updated: 2026-11-01
---

You can delete your Anvi account and the information in it at any time, from inside the app or by email.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open Anvi and sign in.
2. Go to the You tab.
3. Tap "Delete account" and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away, and this cannot be undone. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also removes Anvi's access to your Apple ID. On your phone, the app also clears your quiz result, your daily reminder and your routine checks.

**If you pay for Anvi Plus,** deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it first in your app store's subscription settings.

## What gets deleted

- your account and sign-in details, including your name and email address
- your dosha result and quiz scores
- your wellness goals and dietary preferences
- your time zone and reminder settings
- your entire conversation with Anvi, including menu suggestions saved to it and the summaries Anvi keeps of earlier parts of it
- your usage counts and subscription status
- the record of your subscription purchases and renewals in our database
- the token that lets Anvi use your Apple sign-in

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Request logs.** Our hosting provider, Supabase, keeps a record of each request the app made, including your IP address and, for signed-in requests, your account ID. These are deleted automatically within 7 days.
- **AI usage records.** For each reply or menu scan, we keep a record of which feature was used, its size and its cost, so we can track our costs. These records hold no message text, and your account ID is removed from them when you delete your account.
- **Messages and menu photos already sent to Anthropic** to generate replies. Anthropic deletes them within 30 days. If its safety systems flag a request under its usage policy, it keeps the request for up to 2 years and its safety classification scores for up to 7 years.
- **Purchase records.** Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own records of any purchase under their policies.

## If you no longer have the app

Email privacy@utopianvisions.com from the email address you used with Anvi, with the subject "Delete my account." If you can't send from that address, for example because you used Apple's Hide My Email, tell us the address on the account and we will confirm with you before deleting. We will delete your account and the information listed above within 30 days and reply when it is done.
