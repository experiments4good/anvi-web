Anvi is an Ayurvedic wellness app published by Utopian Visions LLC ("Anvi," "we," "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, how we use it, and the control you have over it. It is written to describe what the app actually does, not to keep our options open.

**The short version.** We collect the account and wellness information you give us so the app can personalize your guidance. Your conversations with Anvi are processed by an AI provider to generate replies. We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

Anvi is a general wellness and educational tool. It is not a medical service, it does not provide medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a licensed healthcare provider or a certified Ayurvedic practitioner. See "Not medical advice" below.

---

## 1. Who this applies to

Anvi is intended for adults. It is available in the United States and Canada. Do not use Anvi if you are under 18.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us:**
- **Account information:** your name and email address when you create an account. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique identifier and, on your first Apple sign-in only, the name you choose to share. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we receive only the relay address.
- **Your wellness profile:** your dosha (constitution) result, your quiz answers, and any wellness goals and dietary preferences you enter.
- **Your conversations:** the messages you send to Anvi and the replies it generates, so your conversation has continuity.
- **Menu photos:** if you use the menu scanner, the photo you take or choose is sent to Anthropic's Claude API so it can read the menu and suggest dishes. We do not store the photo. The suggestions are saved to your conversation. Anthropic deletes API inputs within 30 days, or keeps them for up to 2 years if its safety systems flag a request as violating its usage policy, and does not use them to train its models.

**Information created as you use the app:**
- **Usage counts** needed to run the service, such as how many messages you have used in a day or how many menu scans you have used in the current 30-day period, and your subscription status.
- **Basic technical data** such as error logs, used to keep the app working.

**What we do not collect:** we do not collect your precise location, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

## 3. Where your information is stored and processed

- **Our database and backend** run on Supabase, which hosts your account, your wellness profile, and your conversations. Access is protected by row-level security, so your data is scoped to your account.
- **AI replies** are generated by Anthropic (the Claude API). Your message and the relevant context from your profile and recent conversation are sent to Anthropic to produce each reply. If you use the menu scanner, your menu photo and the relevant context from your profile are sent the same way. Anthropic processes this to return the response and does not use it to train its models on our account. We do not use any Google AI service.
- **Subscriptions** are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which tells us your subscription status so we can unlock paid features. Payment details are handled by the app store; we never see your card number.

## 4. How we use your information

We use your information only to:
- create and secure your account;
- generate your dosha result and personalize the guidance, tips, and conversations you receive;
- operate features you choose to use, including chat and the menu scanner;
- enforce free and paid usage limits and manage your subscription;
- keep the app working, diagnose problems, and prevent abuse;
- contact you about your account or the service when necessary.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with data brokers. We do not use it for advertising or to build a marketing profile of you.

## 5. Legal basis and health information

Some of what you enter (for example a dietary restriction, or something you tell Anvi in conversation) may relate to your health. You provide this by choice, and by using these features you consent to our processing it to give you the wellness guidance you asked for. You can withdraw that consent by deleting your account, which removes the underlying data.

## 6. Your choices and controls

- **Delete your account.** In the app, go to the You tab and tap "Delete account." This permanently removes your profile, your dosha result, your wellness goals and dietary preferences, and your entire conversation with Anvi from our database. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also revokes Anvi's access to your Apple ID. Deletion is not reversible.
- **Edit your information.** You can change your name, goals, dietary preferences, and retake the quiz from the You tab at any time.
- **Sign in with Apple, before this policy.** If you signed in with Apple before token revocation shipped and want to remove Anvi's access, you can do so at any time in your device's Settings under your Apple ID, "Sign in with Apple."
- **Contact us** at the email in Section 11 to ask about the information we hold or to request deletion if you cannot reach the in-app option.

## 7. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your associated data from our database. We may retain a minimal record where the law requires it, for example to meet tax or accounting obligations tied to a purchase, and store or payment providers keep their own transaction records under their policies.

## 8. Security

We protect your information with industry-standard measures: encrypted connections, row-level security scoping data to your account, and access controls on our backend. No system is perfectly secure, but we design Anvi to collect little and to keep what it collects locked to you.

## 9. Children

Anvi is for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app. Continuing to use Anvi after a change means you accept the updated policy.
